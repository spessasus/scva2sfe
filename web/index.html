Sound Canvas VA to SFe converter

Turns the voices of Roland's SOUND Canvas VA into an SFe sound bank, as close to the original as the format allows.

Everything runs in your browser. The files you pick are never uploaded, stored or shared. Only the solve cache below stays in this browser, and you can clear it.

You need your own, legitimate copy of SOUND Canvas VA. This page contains no Roland software or data: the bank is made from the SCCore.dll you pick, and it is for your own use.

The banks use SFe 4 features. They play as intended in spessasynth_core 4.4.11 or newer. Older players load them but miss those features.

Your files

SCCore.dll Required. From the SOUND Canvas VA install folder, version 1.1.6 (x64).
SCVSC.drk Optional. Drum key names of the GS kits ("Closed Hi-Hat 2" instead of the tone's name).
GM2.drk Optional. Drum key names of the GM2 kits.

You can also drop all three files onto the page.

Banks to write

Ogg Vorbis bank: not offered here, browsers have no Vorbis encoder that writes the same bank as the command line converter. Run it with --vorbis for that one.

Pick SCCore.dll first.
